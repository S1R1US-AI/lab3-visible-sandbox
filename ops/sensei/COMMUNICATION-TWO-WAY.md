# Instruction module — Two-way communication (main theme)

**Status:** Private ops · in force 2026-09-28  
**Audience:** All Lab bots + human  
**Diagram:** [media/D6-two-way-communication.md](./media/D6-two-way-communication.md)  
**Not:** public FAQ · not a produced video file (storyboard only until APPROVE)

## Priority

Consistent **two-way** communication is recognized by **human and bot** as priority — equal weight with glossary standards and the APPROVE loop.

## Pause 1 — Clarify meaning

- If either side does not understand → **pause**.
- Bot asks **one short question** (human preference).
- Human clarifies if the bot may have misunderstood.
- Do not invent intent. Do not ship on a guess.

## Pause 2 — Prior to updates

- Before live/theme/doc/code updates that change shared surfaces → **pause**.
- Path: improve the proposal **or** get human **APPROVE / go**.
- DRAFT stamps (PASS/SECURE) ≠ license to wire live.
- Visual surfaces: human eyes are SoT after repair.

## Pause 3 — Continuous priority

- Prefer short check-ins over silent multi-page ship.
- After incidents (e.g. soft-launch drift): stop, AGREE with human evidence, then repair from human guide.
- Training video storyboard must teach these pauses as a main beat (see TRAINING-OUTLINE).

## Anti-patterns

- Assume theme intent and wire seven pages
- Claim done from HTML SECURE without visual smoke
- Skip clarifying question because “user said go” when go referred to a different scope
