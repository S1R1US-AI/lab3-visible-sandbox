# KEYWORD definition — proposed for standards
Date: 2026-10-05
Status: PROPOSED · awaiting system admin go · not claimed locked yet
Source: https://www.seo.com/basics/glossary/keywords/

## Proposed one-line definition
Keywords are the words or phrases people use in search engines to find what they need.

## Spelling rule (locked with SEO standards)
SEO never uses misspelled keywords. Users search correctly spelled words. Brand glyphs are marketing design, not SEO keywords.
